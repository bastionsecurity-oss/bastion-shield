# Code of conduct

Bastion Shield is maintained by Bastion Security GmbH and developed in the open. We want everyone who reports a problem, suggests a filter or sends code to be treated with respect. These rules apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space, and to people representing the project elsewhere. They follow the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## What we expect

- Be friendly and patient, also with people who are new to filter syntax or to open source.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Respect people's privacy: no full URLs with personal data, no screenshots of other people's accounts.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, unwelcome attention.
- Discrimination of any kind, including by origin, language, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age or religion.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other behaviour that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to [info@bastionsecurity.ch](mailto:info@bastionsecurity.ch) with "Code of conduct" in the subject. Every report is reviewed promptly and treated confidentially; we do not share the reporter's identity without consent.

## Enforcement

The maintainers decide on consequences in proportion to the behaviour: a private note, a public warning, a temporary ban from project spaces, or a permanent ban for repeated or severe violations. Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break these rules may be edited, hidden or removed.
