! 20min.ch: the in-feed ad aside is a full-width row of a 5-column teaser grid
! (300 / 14 / 300 / 14 / 300). Hiding it with display:none lets the teasers move
! up a row, and one lands in a 14px divider column. Keep the row, collapse it.
20min.ch#@#section aside:has(> div > div > div[data-ad-unit-id])
